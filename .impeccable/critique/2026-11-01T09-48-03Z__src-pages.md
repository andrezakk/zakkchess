## Design Specificity Verdict
Partly authored, mostly category-interchangeable. The palette, board colours, Pipo with moods and the annotated hero board are this product's own. The page skeletons (hero + form, legend wall, numbered steps, teaser card; KPI tiles + bar panels; price + comparison table) would fit any dark SaaS. The real differentiator, Pipo pointing at YOUR move on YOUR board, appears only in the hero thumbnail and a small bubble below the board; the Report never shows a board.
Deterministic scan: CLI 2 findings in src (side-tab on .game-row border-left 4px, styles.css:340; bounce-easing on .error-mark, styles.css:160) and 8 in built HTML (low-contrast #f6f3ea on #2f7d6d step numbers 4.4:1; dark-glow teal shadow on hero board and focus ring; flat-type-hierarchy advisory on /pro). Browser: undersized UI text on game page (eval label 9.3px, small grade badges 10.6px), oversized h1 on desktop home, stragglers. The focus-ring glow is a false positive.
